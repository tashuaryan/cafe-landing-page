Three things we do differently

Good coffee comes down to sourcing, roasting and brewing. We keep each one simple.

Direct sourcing

We buy from the same six farms each year and pay above fair-trade prices.

Small batches

Our 5 kg roaster lets us tune every origin instead of averaging it out.

Brewed to order

Every cup is weighed, timed and made by hand. No pots sitting on a burner.
